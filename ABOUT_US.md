# 🧑‍🌾 About Farmhouse Fizz

Farmhouse Fizz began in late 2025 as a project between a father and son, built on the idea of creating something meaningful together as a family. Beyond great drinks, the business exists to teach real-world skills like responsibility, teamwork, customer service, and craftsmanship.

We specialize in **Dirty Sodas**, a non-alcoholic drink that originated in Utah. They are made by combining classic sodas with flavored syrups, optional cream, and other mix-ins. It might sound strange at first, but one sip usually changes minds pretty quickly.

---

## 🚚 What We Do

- Mobile soda bar operating from a self-contained concession trailer
- Public events such as fairs, festivals, and street vending
- Private events including weddings, corporate events, school functions, church gatherings, and parties
- Fully customizable drinks made to order

We bring the trailer, the drinks, and the good vibes.
You bring the people.

## 🍒 Why Dirty Sodas?

Dirty Sodas let you customize your drink exactly how you want it:

- Choose your soda or sparkling water base
- Add flavored syrups or fruit
- Include cream, dairy-free alternatives, or none at all
- Adjust sweetness to your taste

It's soda, just upgraded.
